    /ɡɪv weɪ/
    beginner ▼
    Yield to other traffic; let others go first
    让路；让其他车辆先行
    Examples · 例句
    Give way to traffic on the main road.
    让主路车辆先行。
    There's a give way sign at the junction.
    路口有个让行标志。

    In the US, the equivalent sign says 'YIELD'. Both mean the same thing.

  • mind the gap
    /maɪnd ðə ɡæp/
    beginner ▼
    Be careful of the space between the train and the platform
    小心站台与列车之间的空隙
    Examples · 例句
    Mind the gap between the train and the platform.
    请注意列车与站台之间的空隙。
    Mind the gap in your argument.
    注意你论点中的漏洞。
    💡 Cultural Note
